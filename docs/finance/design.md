# PTI Finance design decision

Status **Unverified implementation direction**, 22 November 2026. The finance workbench is a new operating surface inside the existing warm-light PTI identity. This record compares alternatives and gives the implementation brief.

## Scope and evidence

The primary task is to understand a month of recorded personal finances, trace a total to its posted transactions and see which gaps or unresolved entries limit the result. The three-year extraction window is 22 November 2023 through 22 November 2026, inclusive in India local dates. It touches 37 calendar months. November 2023 and November 2026 are partial by date boundary; statement and parsing gaps can affect any month independently. A requested period, scanned period, source statement period, balance date and last successful refresh describe different facts.

## Build brief

- **Navigation.** Keep a roughly 208 px desktop rail, visually docked to the workspace. Put Overview, Transactions and Review first, group Accounts, Income, Investments, Debts and Reports, and keep Sources discoverable. Provide PTI Home as a true link. The rail labels can follow the existing view vocabulary. Keep sign-out available with owner state and clear private rows on loss of access. [Finance view vocabulary](../../finance-app/values.mjs)
- **Overview.** Display the selected month and any narrower known report period with readable dates. Place coverage beside the period or totals, with its affected source count only when known and a link to Sources. Keep six-to-eight immediate comparisons at most between metrics, chart series and categories. Right-align rupee amounts and use tabular numerals. Include recent posted rows and a direct open-transactions action tied to the month. Show a review action when there is a supported queue or actual work remaining.
- **Cash flow and spending.** Use labeled income and spending series on a common zero axis, optionally adding an explicit net series only when the source contract supplies or precisely defines it. Bars above/below zero and labeled series encode direction beyond color. Use actual ordered buckets and independent coverage status. Leave unsupported buckets visibly unplotted and explain the state in an adjacent accessible text summary. Exact amounts and dates must be available without mouse hover. Use a narrow ranked category list with amount and only source-supported denominators. A category or period affordance should open the corresponding ledger scope where supported, with an evident clear action.
- **Transactions.** In the ledger, show date, merchant or source label, account, category/type, posted or review state when relevant, and signed INR amount. Use the actual occurrence date and the source's aliases. Respect pagination and scope. If account, kind, category or search operates only on loaded rows, mark that limit and preserve Load more. Reserve empty filtered-result copy for the population actually searched. Keep applied filters visible and preserve month, filters and list position across details and review actions where possible.
- **Sources and review.** Source family, requested period, observed period, claim status and last known successful refresh deserve their own fields. Treat locked, unparsed, partial and excluded separately, with reason or next legitimate step only when available. For each review, surface the posted/pending/disputed status, recorded date and origin with a compact review/correction action only when the owner endpoint supports it. After confirmation, refresh affected totals and rows. Keep a correction failure or revision conflict explicit and preserve the existing ledger state until it is read back.
- **Money semantics.** Earned income and actual expenses are source-defined. Refunds, reimbursements, card settlements, internal transfers, investment movements, fees and interest each keep their recorded type. Follow the authoritative summary contract for inclusions so a card purchase and its repayment do not double-count. Keep account balance, card amount due and investment value as independently dated snapshots. Avoid deriving household net worth, complete spending, savings rate or trend comparisons from incompatible coverage. Use subordinate qualifiers such as “Recorded in available sources” beside partial amounts.
- **Responsive.** Target 1440×1000 desktop, 1024×768 tablet, 390×844 phone and 320×568 narrow phone. Collapse the rail into a clearly labeled navigation control at small widths. Place month and coverage where they can be read before amounts. Keep the chart axis legible and use a clear list/table alternative on touch and with keyboard. Recompose transactions as readable dated rows with merchant and metadata on the left, signed amount at right, and disclose detail on tap. Keep the search and current filters easy to return to. Preserve target size, visible focus, reading order and wrapped amount/metadata behavior at 200% zoom.

### State handling

| State | Visible behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Loading, including period change | Keep navigation, period and section headings usable. Use a concise textual state and nonnumeric skeletons only where needed. Avoid relabeling prior values with the new period. |
| Recorded data with incomplete coverage | Show real, explicitly observed totals and series, including the affected scope. Keep unopened or unparsed amounts out of aggregate claims. Surface an appropriate Sources action. |
| Confirmed zero or no matching records | Show a numeric zero only for a successful, defined aggregate with sufficient scope. Use “No recorded transactions for this month” when the read returns no records. Use “No transactions match these filters” when a checked filter returns none, with Clear filters. |
| No observations or unresolved section | Show “Unavailable” or the specific reason. An unavailable balance or month has an empty graphic state and the visible data-coverage qualifier. |
| Read failure or refresh timeout | Keep feasible navigation and unaffected sections usable. Show the affected section and Retry. Any retained value must have the same scope and an actual last successful time, and be labeled as such. |
| Access denied or signed out | Use the authorized PTI sign-in or access-denied flow and clear private values, filters containing private data and transaction detail. |
| Long labels and sparse observations | Wrap or disclose names. Keep monetary signs and figures intact at narrow widths. Preserve a readable chart and transaction path with one valid observation. |

## Visual review contract

The intended impression is calm scrutiny. The eye-path hypotheses are a legible month/coverage header at first glance, money in/out and data limitations within a short scan, and a clear ledger or source-review action after reading the overview. These are design hypotheses.

1. **Truth at a glance.** A real partial month visibly distinguishes observed from unknown before the user interprets a total or chart. Zero, unread and filtered-empty states read differently.
2. **PTI fit and visual hierarchy.** Muted warmth and compact section hierarchy carry through without distracting ornament. The month and reported amounts read before supporting metadata; coverage remains visible at that same level of decision.
3. **Ledger traceability.** A period or category selection visibly reaches related evidence with matching scope, legible status and a way back. Reconciliation can be reached and the selected scope is easy to recover.
4. **Narrow readability.** At 390 px and 320 px, controls, signed values, coverage line and chart labels remain legible without document-width overflow, hover dependency or hidden actions.

Independent visual critique and separate practical QA should inspect an exact candidate using the owner's authorized session. Include the monthly overview, filter-to-row flow, review/correction only when authorized and available, coverage gaps, error/empty/loading states available through genuine operation, and long labels. ## Bounded history and report refinement

The Overview history follows the selected India-local ending date through the preceding 36 months. The 22 November 2026 anchor yields 37 calendar buckets, with 22–30 November 2023 and 1–22 November 2026 as the clipped edges. Interior months use the owner-authorized monthly projection. Clipped amounts come only from posted ledger entries within the inclusive dates when the bounded ledger read finishes. The chart keeps earned income, cash debits and net cash movement on one signed scale, with a month list for exact values. Each bucket can open its dated posted ledger, source coverage or calendar-month overview. Coverage badges describe the calendar-month source record. Date-unknown evidence remains available in Sources with its date status visible.

Reports show the current published analysis and its analysis, ledger and reconciliation versions. Linked entries and source windows open owner-readable records. The warm-light Monthly statement direction remains the comparison baseline. The changed Overview, Sources and Reports have an open authenticated desktop and phone visual-verification gate.


## Decision-first revision — 24 November 2026

Owner verdict: the ledger-first interface does not offer enough decision value compared with the banking app and repeated login is unacceptable. Retain the warm-light PTI identity, ledger, sources and reports, but open on a daily cash and decision brief. The three structural alternatives considered were a daily risk/action brief, a calculator-first decision studio and a portfolio dashboard. The first leads, with a dedicated studio for purchase/loan comparisons, confirmed planning assumptions, savings goals, dated bills and a saved decision journal. Exact statement-backed bank movements remain separate from classified consumption. Planning is owner-only, revisioned Firestore data and not a modification of the bank ledger. Unconfirmed inputs, stale balances and source gaps block an unconditional affordability claim. Cash/loan comparison is an amortizing fixed-rate approximation, not a bank offer. No additional schedules.

The baseline is retained in Git; the previous detailed overview remains navigable. This change's actual desktop/mobile rendering and owner-browser sign-in/save interaction still require verification. Independent visual acceptance cannot be inferred from source or deployment.
