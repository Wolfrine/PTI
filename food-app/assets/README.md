# Food artwork

The bhel, vada-pav, dal, hara-bhara, paneer-tikka and veg-biryani images were generated with the built-in image tool on 2026-10-01, then exported as 768px WebP assets. They are generic illustrative food artwork, not photographs from restaurants and not evidence of exact ingredients, serving sizes, prices or current availability.

Shared prompt: one square editorial culinary photograph-style illustration of the named vegetarian dish on handmade ceramic, warm parchment table, natural daylight, cream/moss/terracotta palette, no text/branding/people/meat/fish/eggs. Dish-specific subjects: wet Mumbai bhel with puffed rice/sev/tamarind; pav around a potato fritter and garlic chutney; golden lentil dal fry with tempering; green spinach/pea hara bhara patties.

Additional subjects: dry grilled paneer tikka with charred corners and chutney; long-grain vegetable biryani with vegetables and caramelised onions. Use only with matching dish names. Unsupported foods retain native illustrations rather than borrowing an unrelated photograph. Original generated files are retained outside the source repository. Existing meal.webp is a legacy asset.
