# Morsel — living food atelier

Date: 2026-10-01. Level: L3 consumer app, selective expressive motion. Owner authorized a substantial better version following the preceding visual/product audits and Central's designer–critic loop.

Intent: relief from food indecision, recognition without invented certainty, agency in shaping the meal, appetite and quiet delight. Feelings are design hypotheses to validate, not claims about the user's mind.

Current → intended: attractive hero plus preference form / dense secondary screens → a useful, manipulable meal object and contextual choices; sensory palette in Taste; personal journal in History; small evidence-backed tasting flight in Discover.

Direction alternatives: photographic supper club (appetising but needs accurate, consistent dish assets); editorial food journal (personal and restrained but weak playful agency); living pixel atelier (food itself responds to choices, with precise facts nearby). Select living pixel atelier with editorial restraint. A recoloured hero does not satisfy the change.

Reference mechanisms already observed/retrieved: Material—visible cause/effect and object continuity; Linear—prune until the next action is apparent; Collection—content carries identity; Atum—tangible object metaphor with precise controls; Butter—direct manipulation of objects; KILTER type playground—subject becomes actual interactive material; KANOE atelier—consistent palette, typography and sensory imagery rather than isolated decoration. Do not copy palettes, layouts, incense metaphors or spectacular motion that obstructs a meal decision.

Relevant Central entry points: README, UI_AGENT_PROTOCOL, EXECUTION_ROUTING, DESIGN_REVIEW_LOOP. Critic also loads VISUAL_CRITIC, LAYERED_DESIGN_AUDIT and PERCEPTUAL_DESIGN_FOUNDATIONS; GENERIC_AI_UI is the anti-pattern check. Both assigned agents must acknowledge reading applicable guidance.

Product/data contract: standalone Morsel PWA; existing owner-scoped Firestore/Gmail ingestion/MCP; no new account, provider credential, polling or Dot. Preserve receipts, explicit consumed-dish learning, plans, user-authored memories, sourced menus, uncertainty and inspection. Receipt repeat is not proven affection; current profile contains 93 historical orders and only one confirmed dish reaction. Keep private records/fixtures/screenshots out of GitHub. Exclusions persist through discovery, feedback drafts survive rerenders and semantic dish aliases must not produce duplicate choices.

Acceptance: useful meal/action without a giant introductory screen; actual dishes determine visual meal material; intelligible controls; compact integrated interpretation; different rhythms for all four screens; smartphone composition intact at 390 and 320 px; clear saved-plan continuity; honest evidence; reduced motion and keyboard/focus; no regressions in critical flows. At least five substantive independent rendered-review cycles, score >=8/10, and no unresolved material defects before completion.

Roles: designer subagent owns app.mjs, index.html, styles.css and any new native visual module; parent owns intelligence.mjs, its tests, review harness and release; independent critic reviews renders/interactions and returns scores plus concrete changes. Designer receives concise text feedback. Parent integrates and deploys only after the gate.

## Accepted result

Six substantive independent cycles pass the Central gate. Actual meals carry the visual identity and direct controls; the saved direction remains near the top and can be edited in place. Taste distinguishes stated preferences, reacted-dish name interpretations and unconfirmed receipt clues. History is a journal; Discover is a small sourced tasting flight with the original constraints preserved. Changed food exits/arrives briefly; unchanged pieces stay still. Keyboard, reduced motion, modal errors, retry and draft preservation are part of the accepted experience.

Agent scores are expert judgment, not a user study or proof of intended emotions. The owner remains the final taste authority. Evidence uses the existing account history in an isolated local Firebase facade; no review action changes cloud records. Release checks separately validate Hosting assets and account access. Native illustrations are family-level interpretations, not dish photography or ingredient/portion evidence. Current prices and availability still require menu inspection; the deterministic model and existing Gmail event infrastructure are unchanged in scope.
