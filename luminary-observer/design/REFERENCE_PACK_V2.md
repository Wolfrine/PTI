# Luminary Reference Pack v2

Status: Used for Trace Register direction
Observed: 2026-09-23

This pack records mechanisms, not a moodboard. Only behavior visible in the cited source or explicitly described by the source is recorded. Unseen responsive and motion states are not invented.

## 1. Dear Data

Source: https://giorgialupi.com/dear-data
Type: personal data / editorial / analog visualization

Observed:
- A year-long project built from fifty-two weekly hand-drawn data postcards.
- Each week begins with noticing and recording a specific slice of life, then translating it into a visual notation.
- Each drawing carries a detailed key so the marks remain interpretable.
- The creators describe the project as a personal documentary rather than quantified-self optimization.
- Physical scuffs and accumulated marks remain part of the artifact.

Entry experience:
- The project is introduced through the act and ritual of noticing rather than a performance metric.

Composition:
- Data marks dominate. Explanation is secondary and explicit.

Typography:
- Typography behaves as annotation / legend rather than brand spectacle.

Imagery:
- The data drawing itself is the primary image.

Motion / interaction:
- Not relied on for the core idea; accumulation across weeks supplies the temporal behavior.

Responsive:
- Not used as evidence for Luminary; no mobile behavior was inferred.

Distinctive mechanism:
- Personal observation becomes a trace with a readable grammar.

Transfer to Luminary:
- Treat capture as leaving a trace in a longitudinal record.
- Keep visual keys and epistemic labels explicit.
- Let accumulation, not decoration, create richness.

Do not copy:
- Hand-drawn postcard styling, bespoke symbols, or scrapbook materiality.

Applicability: high

## 2. Histography

Source: https://histography.io/
Type: interactive temporal visualization

Observed:
- Every dot represents a historical event sourced from Wikipedia.
- Time is the dominant spatial coordinate.
- A bottom range control changes the visible period.
- Selected events move into a focused reading area.

Entry experience:
- The interface immediately establishes the temporal field and explains how to change scale.

Composition:
- A broad time field is primary; detail is subordinate.

Typography:
- Small labels support a large spatial visualization.

Imagery:
- Events are reduced to marks until selected.

Motion / interaction:
- Zooming / resizing the visible time span changes density and context.

Responsive:
- No mobile behavior was used as evidence.

Distinctive mechanism:
- One persistent coordinate system can hold dense history and focused detail without converting everything into cards.

Transfer to Luminary:
- Use time as the stable geometry of the product.
- Let the same coordinate persist from capture to longitudinal review.

Do not copy:
- Dot-cloud treatment, history taxonomy, or particle-like field.

Applicability: high

## 3. Are.na

Source: https://www.are.na/about
Type: shipped knowledge product / quiet interface

Observed:
- The product is explicitly about saving content, creating collections over time and connecting ideas.
- It positions itself as a mindful space for working through projects over time.
- The product language prioritizes utility and accumulation rather than algorithmic spectacle.

Entry experience:
- Direct product language and restrained interface conventions.

Composition:
- Information is allowed to remain information; hierarchy does not depend on decorative surfaces.

Typography:
- Functional, low-theatre text hierarchy.

Imagery:
- Content is the material rather than a decorative background system.

Motion / interaction:
- No motion mechanism was taken from the About page.

Responsive:
- No unseen responsive behavior was inferred.

Distinctive mechanism:
- Restraint and system clarity can feel intentional when the content model itself is strong.

Transfer to Luminary:
- Use native-feeling controls and minimal chrome around capture.
- Avoid using visual effects as proof of depth.

Do not copy:
- Channel / block information architecture or Are.na's literal UI skin.

Applicability: medium-high

## 4. Daylight Computer

Reference: https://www.a1.gallery/website/daylight
Product: https://daylightcomputer.com/
Type: calm commercial product / hardware

Observed from the reference:
- Light backgrounds and large unused areas carry the calmness.
- Product imagery and typography do most of the visual work.
- The design does not need a dark futuristic field to communicate a different relationship with computing.

Entry experience:
- Clear product object plus generous quiet space.

Composition:
- Sparse, asymmetrical, materially light.

Typography:
- Direct and modern rather than ornamental.

Imagery:
- Product / physical context is central.

Motion / interaction:
- The reference documents scroll animation; no specific unseen choreography is borrowed.

Responsive:
- Not used as evidence.

Distinctive mechanism:
- Calmness can come from luminance, pacing and absence rather than darkness.

Transfer to Luminary:
- Permit a warm-light instrument field.
- Use one restrained signal accent against an otherwise neutral surface.

Do not copy:
- Hardware photography, nature positioning, or ecommerce CTA language.

Applicability: medium

## 5. The Pudding — Can Data Die?

Source: https://pudding.cool/2021/10/lenna/
Type: editorial / data story

Observed:
- Editorial text and visual evidence are composed as one reading system.
- The page uses the visual material being investigated as structural evidence rather than as decoration.
- Context is revealed alongside the evidence instead of hiding the argument inside generic cards.

Entry experience:
- Strong editorial framing before deeper evidence.

Composition:
- Copy and evidence alternate in hierarchy; graphics carry semantic weight.

Typography:
- Editorial scale creates narrative hierarchy.

Imagery:
- Image evidence is part of the argument.

Motion / interaction:
- Interaction supports inspection / context; it is not treated as ambient spectacle.

Responsive:
- No mobile behavior was inferred here.

Distinctive mechanism:
- The visualization is meaningful because it is the evidence.

Transfer to Luminary:
- Geometry on Patterns must correspond to actual supporting observations or remain absent.
- Never invent attractive positions for derived evidence.

Do not copy:
- The Lenna image motif, purple palette, or editorial story structure.

Applicability: high for epistemic discipline

## 7. Prague Quadrennial archive

Official archive: https://services.pq.cz/en/archive.html
Awwwards discovery reference: https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/archive-prague-quadrennial-pq-2023
Type: cultural archive / temporal navigation

Observed:
- The archive is organized by historical editions.
- The Awwwards reference identifies an interactive timeline / archive treatment as a defining part of the experience.
- Navigation treats historical sequence as a spatial relationship rather than a stack of unrelated cards.

Entry experience:
- Archive identity is established before individual historical details.

Composition:
- Temporal sequence is a navigable field.

Typography:
- Labels provide orientation inside a larger spatial system.

Imagery:
- Historical material is attached to sequence rather than used as ambient decoration.

Motion / interaction:
- Timeline navigation is the useful mechanism; WebGL itself is not the lesson.

Responsive:
- No mobile behavior was inferred.

Distinctive mechanism:
- Archive navigation can preserve spatial continuity across time.

Transfer to Luminary:
- Desktop can use width to express temporal relationships while mobile uses the same grammar vertically.

Do not copy:
- Exhibition imagery, WebGL spectacle, or the archive's visual styling.

Applicability: medium-high

## Extracted design learnings

1. Time can be the primary geometry, not a label attached to cards.
2. Accumulation can create visual richness without gamification.
3. A mark becomes trustworthy when its visual position corresponds to real recorded evidence.
4. Quiet does not require darkness, glow or blur.
5. Annotation and explicit keys improve epistemic clarity.
6. Desktop should expose temporal relationships; mobile should preserve capture speed.
7. The same visual object can transform across states: present-time register -> saved trace -> longitudinal recurrence.
8. If evidence is missing, show the absence instead of synthesizing an attractive visualization.

## Anti-pattern check

Explicitly rejected:
- dark + cyan / violet / mint glow;
- orbits / concentric consciousness geometry;
- neural / constellation pattern graphs;
- decorative particles;
- glass surfaces;
- bento composition;
- rounded-card accumulation;
- premium serif as an identity shortcut;
- pill CTA;
- fake temporal positions;
- motion that exists only as fade / scale theatre.

## Resulting question

What could still be recognized as Luminary with its name removed?

Answer used for the next prototype:

A continuous observation register in which the present is a coordinate, capture leaves a trace, and derived recurrence is shown only where supporting observations actually exist in time.
