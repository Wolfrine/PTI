# Personal / design contract

L2 editorial intelligence journal. Read the relevant Central/design protocol, execution routing and perceptual foundations; do not copy their corpus. Scope is Personal only.

Thesis: source visual → claim/evidence → your response → dated reflection. A finite edition, not an administrative dashboard or an endless engagement feed. Paper/ink/rust, serif article hierarchy, one lead story and smaller subsequent entries. Proximity keeps feedback with its article; containment separates evidence from interpretation. Native disclosures progressively reveal detail; short press/selection transitions communicate state. Reduced motion preserves all information.

The owner explicitly requested imagery; the old text-only decision is superseded. Load relevant source images with provenance and no referrer. Generated illustrations must be labelled and must point to an actual asset. UI-native concept sketches are a clearly labelled fallback, not event photos or claims of image-model generation. No decorative orbit/glow/network metaphor. No unsourced outlet trust score.

Today, Capture, Threads and Reflection are distinct tasks. Saving a source is not an inferred endorsement. Missing reaction means unknown. Reflections cite explicit source records, show partial-day status, and are hidden after a supporting response changes or is deleted. Private captures are excluded from agent interpretation.

Rendered-browser fallback: actual modules bundled with synthetic demo state were inspected at 1440, 768, 390 and 360px. The mobile masthead was too tall; reduced it and reran flows. Tested evidence expansion, reactions, shared note, private capture, source-to-thread and stale-reflection hiding. CI additionally serves the real built modules and stores screenshots. Live deployment evidence is the workflow result, not this note. No user records are copied into fixtures.

Acceptance: a visual per story; readable image credit and claim classification; source/limitations disclosure; 44px primary tap controls; labelled forms/focus; mobile navigation; no overflow; finite ending; reflection traceability; explicit empty/error/sample states; no private response caching. The owner remains final taste authority.
