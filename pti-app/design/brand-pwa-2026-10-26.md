# PTI identity and PWA launch artwork — 26 Oct 2026

Status: **implementation candidate; no post-release owner acceptance**.

## Scope and finding

The browser favicon had been refreshed, but `manifest.webmanifest` had no `icons` entries. Installed launcher art and Android's manifest-generated launch screen therefore had no defined modern PTI icon. Keep the owner-selected warm editorial Home/login design and real sign-in behavior.

## Visual decision

Keep the established fourfold/petal identity for continuity. Independent designer compared three unrelated directions (segmented ring, sundial, serif lettermark) with the current emblem at 512, 32 and 16 pixels; an independent critic found none stronger. Two continuity-preserving revisions followed. **Fourfold Iris** was chosen for its sculptural clockwise petals, alternating coral/terracotta and motion while remaining recognizably PTI. The center gaps were widened; the browser favicon has a 1.15× optical enlargement for clarity at 16px.

Critical checks: identifiable at 16/32px; crafted at 192/512px; mark stays inside the centered 80%-diameter maskable safe zone; matches the existing warm-light identity. Desktop/mobile page functionality, icon masks and OS splash are separate QA states. The mock splash used for direction is *not* a real Android launch capture.

## One identity, all surfaces

| Surface | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Browser SVG favicon and multiresolution ICO | `public/favicon.svg`, `public/favicon.ico` |
| Installed Android/desktop PWA | `public/icons/icon-app.svg` → opaque `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png` |
| Adaptive Android icon | `public/icons/icon-maskable.svg` → `icon-maskable-512.png`, opaque full-bleed background, padded mark |
| iOS home screen | `public/icons/apple-touch-icon.png`, linked in `index.html` |
| Login/header/Home marks and first-paint loading state | `public/icons/pti-mark.svg` |

The manifest declares explicit icon purposes, stable identity/scope and ivory theme/background. Android constructs its native install splash from the manifest name, icon and colors; PTI's brief inline startup state provides matching branding only while Angular boots. No artificial loading delay is introduced.
