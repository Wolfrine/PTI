# PTI Home — local companion status rail

Owner evidence (7 November 2026): the compact Resource Pulse was liked, but the separate GT Papers and One Piece status cards were too large and visually inconsistent. The established warm, quiet Home identity must remain.

## Bounded choice

- Separate smaller cards: simple, but still repetitive and space-hungry.
- **One two-service rail:** selected; one light frame, two horizontal segments on desktop, concise stacked rows on mobile.
- Minimal status pills: small but conceal Start, Refresh and useful offline context.

The rail preserves live labels and source-specific detail, Start/Refresh/Open actions, and the hidden status for users without access. Healthy details remain one line; important warning/starting instructions wrap. Dividers follow the actual container width, not a guessed viewport, and mobile controls have 44px touch targets. No new data listeners or polling were introduced.

Independent source review preferred the integrated rail and identified three corrections (mid-width dividers, truncated warnings, undersized touch controls), all incorporated before release. Source comparison found improved compactness and cohesion with unchanged service operations. Final owner acceptance and live mobile rendering remain to be verified.
