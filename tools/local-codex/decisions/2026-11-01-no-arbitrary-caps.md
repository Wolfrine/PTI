# Withdraw unapproved execution ceilings

Owner instruction, 2026-11-01: remove the imposed Local Codex limits; do not be conservative without a reason, and ask beforehand when imposing a restriction. The earlier generated three-parent, two-child, one-native-layer and thirty-minute defaults were design choices without measured justification, not approved owner limits. They are withdrawn.

## Contract

`maxConcurrentTasks`, `maxSubagentsPerTask` and `maxTaskMinutes` default to null: no bridge-imposed ceiling. Null is not converted to a larger finite number. Explicit single-agent task routing and explicitly owner-selected finite settings remain supported. New or lower harness restrictions require prior explicit owner approval. The one-improvement-per-review ceiling is also withdrawn, along with the one-hundred-continuation and twenty-human-response rejection gates. Pending human requests are not rejected for exceeding the old ten-entry report bound.

The hosted admission schema, capacity/status arithmetic and durable claim recovery support an uncapped 0.4.2 worker. Old workers keep their actual protocol capabilities until upgraded. Existing queue query pagination is a transport batch, not an active parent limit; further pages are claimed on subsequent polls. There is no capacity-count rejection when the parent ceiling is null. Genuine native-thread and shared Git/workspace ownership conflicts remain separate from numerical admission. Cancellation, lease identity, explicit human decisions and exact Sol/xhigh verification are preserved.

The worker adds no native child-count override when the bridge setting is null. Official OpenAI subagent documentation says omission lets Codex choose its own default; it does not document an unlimited sentinel. Native Codex/account limits therefore remain platform-managed and must be reported accurately. No arbitrary replacement number or global native-config change is introduced. Bridge nesting rejection is removed. Model observation follows descendants beyond the earlier sixteen-child telemetry ceiling; missing execution details remain unknown.

Default duration creates no execution deadline. A finite owner deadline is checked on the existing control timer, avoiding a large setTimeout value overflowing into an immediate interruption. Health/lease/network timeouts detect failed transport and do not constitute a total work-duration budget. Existing running workers retain their loaded settings until completion or explicit continuation; do not cancel/restart owner work to change those settings.
