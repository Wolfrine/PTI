# Design Direction

- First-glance hierarchy: current discovery objective, external signals, shortlist.
- Calm light surfaces; avoid generic dark AI dashboards, neon gradients, glassmorphism and bento-for-its-own-sake.
- Use borders and spatial grouping to imply evidence relationships only where the data supports them.
- Desktop: persistent left rail + split evidence/detail views.
- Mobile: bottom navigation, single-column evidence/detail flow, minimal scroll overhead.
- Motion is intentionally limited to small hover/state transitions; this is an analysis tool, not a showcase surface.
