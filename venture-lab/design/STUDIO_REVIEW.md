## Intent and review basis

The distinctive act is an original discussion becoming several inspectable directions, each carrying its evidence and decision history into a test. A connection must represent a saved source or parent relationship. Selection should keep the conversation, claim and source in the same working context. A stock metric dashboard, decorative mind map or fake agent activity would fail the product-meaning gate.

References used as mechanisms: Linear's workflow grouping and low chrome; Collection's idea-first labeling; Seasats' invariant comparison fields; Material's containment and object continuity; MagicPath's visible artifact-bound work/provenance. Their palettes, shapes and decorative motifs are not requirements. The previous Evidence Desk had useful restrained foundations but its outside-in three-stage model does not cover shared exploration.

## Acceptance scenarios

| Scenario | Required observed result |
|---|---|
| Entry / empty | Google sign-in and isolated, labeled sample are distinct. An empty workspace invites the first discussion without suggesting data exists. |
| Shared discussion | Original text and author remain visible. Additions/corrections preserve history. Background changes do not erase a draft. Voice controls expose permission/unsupported state and retain playable original audio. |
| Branching | Create at least two directions from a discussion; identify their origin and active direction immediately. Parent/child relationships match stored records; mobile can inspect one direction without navigating a miniature canvas. |
| Compare / decide | Same seven fields are comparable; unknowns remain unknown. A reason is required for the decision, with revisit trigger available; rejected/parked alternatives and the decision history remain reachable. |
| Research | Focused question enters an honest queued state. Findings expose stance, evidence type, author and source beside the claim. Challenges receive equal legibility. New outside discovery remains possible. |
| Experiment | Define hypothesis, method and success criterion, then record result, learning and next action. Outcome does not silently promote a branch. |
| Shared access | Owner-only member management; share link distinguishes choosing a workspace from granting access. No unrequested message is sent. Non-owner role and errors remain clear. |
| Phone / accessibility | Entry, thread, compare, research and experiment preserve action priority at phone width. No clipping/obscured control. Keyboard/focus, dialog dismissal, error states and reduced-motion behavior are tested. |
