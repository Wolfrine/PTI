## Layered outcome

- Typography: **replace** old generic serif/sans pairing → one operational sans family + monospace evidence metadata.
- Color: **refine** warm base; assign saturation only to real stage/state meaning.
- Layout/composition: **replace** repeated card dashboard → asymmetric evidence desk + indexed records + dossier.
- Surface language: **replace** rounded SaaS cards → flat records, evidence regions and explicit stage edges.
- Information hierarchy: **retain/refine** objective-first structure; make shortlist the payoff.
- Interaction/affordance: **refine** selection continuity and mobile reorder.
- Motion: **refine** to state-change animation only; reduced-motion supported.
- Product metaphor: **replace** vague “market ledger” styling → explicit evidence transformation system.
- Responsive: **replace** simple stacking → selected dossier becomes primary on mobile.
