The rejected implementation repeats names, dates and monograms over a prolonged sparse public page. Its family overview gives large decorative/date and explanatory areas attention that should belong to actual tasks. It also exposes broad record editing and duplicates decisions across views. Botanical refinement must address these structural failures, not simply recolour the same layout or add a floral corner.
