# Guest Experience — A Living Invitation

## Intent

A personal, beautiful invitation to the wedding of **Mahima Mishra and Mayank Awasthi**, helping guests feel welcome and understand the celebration without repeatedly contacting the family.

The public experience can express the couple's story and invitation-card details: events, dates and start times, a countdown to a specifically confirmed moment, venues and directions, and practical attendance information.

## Known versus ready to publish

The [Wedding Record](../WEDDING_RECORD.md) identifies **18–20 January 2027** in *initial catering notes*, including a **Sangeet evening on 18 January**. The Reception menu is **undated**. These are **planning inputs**, not invitation-approved public details.

Until the invitation or family confirms them, **do not assume** the exact wedding/Reception dates or times, venue, location, event audience, countdown start/target or final programme. A countdown must not silently assume midnight. Food lists, costs, family responsibilities, room allocations and other private planning information are not public invitation content.

## Experience boundary

Guests encounter an inviting, simple celebration experience rather than a family administration surface. Public information should be confirmed, relevant and usable on a phone. The family workspace requires authorization, independent of access to the guest invitation.
