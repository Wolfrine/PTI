# Guest Experience — A Living Invitation

## Intent

An inviting, public space that conveys the character of the wedding and answers guests' essential questions naturally.

The invitation's information should feel alive and easy to use: **whose celebration it is; the dates and times; anticipation through a countdown; venues and their locations; the flow of festivities; and other relevant invitation details**.

Guests should know where to be, when to arrive, and what to expect without searching through messages or asking family members repeatedly. The tone should be warm and personal, particularly for guests viewing the experience on a phone.

## Experience boundary

This is the face of the celebration, not an administrative interface. It is accessible without a family login. Only appropriate, confirmed, guest-facing information belongs here.

The actual invitation card and confirmed event information—not assumptions—define the personal details.
